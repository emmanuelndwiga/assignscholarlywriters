What you can report

If it happened to you online, it counts.

Online harassment

Threats, abuse, or repeated unwanted contact across platforms.

Non-consensual image sharing

Intimate images or videos shared, or threatened to be shared, without your consent.

Cyberstalking

Being tracked, monitored, or repeatedly contacted against your will.

Impersonation

Someone using your name, photos, or identity without your permission.

Doxxing

Your private information exposed publicly to intimidate or endanger you.

Sextortion

Being threatened or coerced using intimate images or information.

Something else

Not seeing it here doesn't mean it doesn't matter. Tell us in your own words.
